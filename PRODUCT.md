# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Kids and teens playing in a browser, on school or family laptops (often a trackpad, sometimes keyboard only) and on phones in landscape. Many are new to first-person shooters; they want to get into a match quickly and to show off their character.

## Product Purpose

CLAYFRONT is an original first-person shooter with clay-figure characters, played free in the browser with no install. Success: a new player understands the controls within a minute, gets into a match in one or two clicks, and enjoys customizing and showing their character.

## Positioning

Every character, weapon and map is sculpted from clay-like shapes in code, so the game's whole world looks handmade and toy-like while playing like a real shooter. It runs from a web page on ordinary devices.

## Operating Context

- Home screen: a 3D showcase of the player's character on a turntable on a small beach island, with mode choices beside it.
- Modes: Battle Royale (1 player + 49 AI bots), Gun Game, Custom Match (rooms with 6-letter join codes and invite links), and an interactive 14-step Training.
- Screens: Characters (15 characters, color and gear customization), Loadouts (5 slots), Armory, Profile, Statistics, Settings, How to Play, Results.
- Input: mouse and keyboard, keyboard only, or touch with on-screen buttons.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static files on GitHub Pages (`/game/`), plain HTML/CSS/JS modules with three.js; no build step, no server.
- Must stay fast on low-end laptops and phones.
- Text must be large enough to read on large monitors, laptops and phones; the interface scales with the screen.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: CLAYFRONT. Default hero: Agent 18594.
- Clay-figure look for characters and weapons.
- All characters, maps, logos and skins are original; no Call of Duty or other franchise assets.

## Evidence on Hand

No press, reviews, player counts or testimonials exist. Never invent them. Bots are always labeled as bots, and simulated matches are never presented as online multiplayer.

## Product Principles

1. Play first: the path from opening the page to being in a match is short and obvious.
2. Readable at a glance: large type and clear contrast beat dense information.
3. Friendly, not childish; playful, not military.
4. One thing at a time: avoid cluttered screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Young players: plain words, large touch targets, readable contrast, keyboard-only and touch play supported, reduced-motion respected.
