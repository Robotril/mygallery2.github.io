# CLAYFRONT home screen and in-game HUD

Scope: the game's menus (home, mode setup, rooms, characters, loadouts, armory, profile, stats, settings, how to play, results, pause) and the in-match HUD. Visitor mode: Experience (the character and the game lead), with Operate-grade clarity on every control.

Audience: kids and teens on laptops (trackpad or keyboard only) and phones in landscape. Job: pick a mode and play within one or two clicks; show off and customize the character. Must not feel cluttered, generic-military, or dark and hard to read.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The interface is made of the same modeling clay as the characters. Every panel is a thick, hand-pressed slab of bright plasticine sitting on the sunny beach diorama. It refuses the gray-and-orange tactical HUD and the neon esports lobby.

OWN-WORLD: Light scene (daytime beach, indoor laptop use). Slabs in saturated clay colors with a fine thumbprint grain, soft inner highlight and shade, slightly uneven hand-made corners. Mode colors: Battle Royale tangerine, Gun Game teal, Custom Match sky blue, How to Play sunflower; neutral slabs in cool clay white; ink is deep navy, never pure black. Type: Baloo 2 ExtraBold for display, looking pressed into the clay; Nunito for UI text. Icons are one authored SVG set with thick rounded strokes. No emoji, no glass, no gradients as decoration.

STORY: Opening the game, a player sees their clay hero and three big clay slabs. They tap the biggest one and are in a match, or press their character and start customizing. Everything after that uses the same slabs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: CLAYFRONT wordmark pressed into a small slab, then a large tangerine Battle Royale slab with a Play action, then Gun Game and Custom Match slabs side by side, then a row of round clay buttons (How to Play, Characters, Loadouts, Armory, Settings). Right: the 3D hero on its turntable, large, with a small slab showing the player's name and level. One quiet daily-challenge chip. Nothing else.

FORM: Plasticine Tray, position 1 on my ordered list (chosen by the user as the pick over the rolled direction Match Day Kit, position 5). seed:0c0f71e53f3e667b. Signature interaction: slabs drop onto the screen and squash on landing; every button squashes when pressed and springs back.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
